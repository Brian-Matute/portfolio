Bio

Meet Brian

I'm a bilingual broadcast journalist based in Miami and a 2026 graduate of the University of Miami, where I earned a B.S. in Communication with a major in Broadcast Journalism.

I spent my college years at UMTV, the university's student TV station. I started as an anchor and reporter on NewsBreak, then became Associate Producer and finally Executive Producer. I also reported live for the award-winning newscast NewsVision, covered entertainment and lifestyle for Pulse, and ran the audio board for live broadcasts. In May 2026 I received the Andrew Barton Award for Outstanding Commitment to UMTV.

"Accurate, engaging, impactful journalism, held to the highest standards of integrity."
